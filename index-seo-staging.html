Frequently Asked Questions

Who is NGCC for?

NGCC is exclusively for registered federal contractors.

What information is required to start?

Name, business name, and email address. Available federal contractor capability information and NAICS classifications support dashboard creation.

What is included free?

A personalized dashboard, five highest-rated current federal matches, estimated match ratings, filters, and official solicitation links.

Does a match guarantee eligibility or award?

No. Contractors remain responsible for reviewing the complete official solicitation and verifying all requirements.
