Understand the Match Before You Pursue

Understand the match before you pursue

Match ratings provide decision support by showing how closely an opportunity appears to align with the contractor’s business profile and available procurement information.

Match ratings support prioritization

Ratings help contractors compare opportunities and decide where deeper review is justified. They do not replace the official solicitation or the contractor’s independent eligibility review.

StrongHigher apparent alignment
ModeratePotential alignment requiring review
LimitedLower apparent alignment or missing information

Optional filters refine the dashboard

  • NAICS or capability area
  • Set-aside category
  • Agency or department
  • Response deadline
  • Place of performance
  • Opportunity status
Analyze Fit Report

Move from a promising match to a clearer pursuit decision

An additional Analyze Fit Report provides a deeper review of one selected opportunity for a one-time price of $15.

What the report may evaluate

  • Capability and scope alignment
  • Eligibility and registration considerations
  • NAICS, set-aside, and certification relevance
  • Deadline and submission-readiness factors
  • Execution complexity and apparent pursuit risk
  • Recommended next review actions

Decision support—not a guarantee

The report helps the contractor understand the opportunity before committing significant time and resources. Contractors must review the official solicitation and remain responsible for their final bid decision, eligibility determination, and submission.

Additional Analyze Fit Report: $15 one-time. No contract award is guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about NGCC access and services

Who is NGCC designed to serve?

NGCC is designed for registered federal contractors seeking a personalized federal procurement experience. State-only licensed contractors are served through NAT-CORP rather than NGCC.

What information do I provide first?

The guided experience begins with your name, business name, and email address. Your Procurement Advisor then explains the next steps used to confirm contractor identity and build your Business Capability Profile.

How much does NGCC cost?

NGCC includes a 14-day free trial. The subscription is $99 per month after the trial and remains active monthly until canceled.

Can I cancel during the trial or before my next billing date?

Yes. You may cancel before the next billing date. After a paid renewal, access continues through the current paid billing period.

What is an Analyze Fit Report?

An Analyze Fit Report is an optional, deeper evaluation of one selected opportunity. Each additional report costs $15 as a one-time charge.

Does a match rating guarantee eligibility or a contract award?

No. Match ratings provide decision support only. Contractors must review the official solicitation, verify their eligibility, and make their own pursuit decision. No contract award is guaranteed.

Is proposal development included in the monthly subscription?

No. Contract Proposal Development is separately priced based on the selected opportunity and required scope of work.

Is NGCC part of the federal government or SAM.gov?

No. NGCC is operated by APROPOS Group LLC. It is not a government agency and is not affiliated with or endorsed by SAM.gov.
